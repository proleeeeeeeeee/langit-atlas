本项目取自 GFZ 原始浮点 GeoTIFF，仅保留两个马来西亚区域。每次坐标查询使用原图
30 角秒网格，赤道附近每格约 0.93 公里。2016 年发表的模型以 2014 年 VIIRS 夜光
数据为基础，表示晴朗条件下天顶的人造光亮度，单位为毫坎德拉每平方米（mcd/m²）。
数值经过对数量化，正数的编码相对误差小于 4.2%。模型参考天然天光为
0.174 mcd/m²。人工亮度除以这个参考值，即为人工亮度相对天然参考天光的比例。
零输出不适合作为地点天光极暗的证据，网站将其标记为缺少可用估值。

This adaptation crops the original GeoTIFF to two rectangles, keeps native
30 arcsecond source cells, logarithmically quantizes positive model luminance
(encoding error under 4.2% for these crops), and creates two colour-mapped,
Web Mercator PNG overlays. Source zero results and source nodata are treated
as unavailable in point queries. Model inputs are a 2014 VIIRS composite.
The model estimates artificial zenith luminance under reference clear-sky
conditions. It cannot report the night's weather, moonlight, an actual SQM
meter reading, a Bortle observation, or a probability of seeing the Milky Way.

The cartography crops and simplifies Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 Countries,
