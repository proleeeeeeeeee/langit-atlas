Langit Atlas selected all 523 non-solar catalogue entries with apparent
visual magnitude at or brighter than 4.00. Each JSON row contains
[right ascension in degrees, declination in degrees, apparent visual magnitude].
The coordinates use epoch and equinox J2000.0. Right ascension was converted
from hours to degrees by multiplying by 15. Both angles were rounded to four
decimal places. Rows are sorted by apparent magnitude, then right ascension.
Lower magnitude values mean brighter catalogue stars. Variable-star magnitudes
use the source catalogue value. Local horizon, weather and time are applied
separately when rendering. A source row with id 0 (the Sun) was excluded.

星表取自 David Nash（Astronexus）编制的 HYG 4.1。星野手记筛选了 523 颗视星等
不大于 4.00 的恒星，并转换、舍入其坐标。星表和该子集均依 CC BY-SA 4.0 授权。
每行依次为 J2000.0 赤经（度）、赤纬（度）、目视星等。数值越小，星表中的恒星越亮。
